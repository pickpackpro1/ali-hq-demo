Models & routing

Every model in the Higgsfield API catalog (84 workflows, copied from the official docs on 29 Oct 2026), grouped by the job it does. The router picks from here and explains why; you can override any step before approving.

How the router decides
For each step it asks: still image, video or speech? New, or fixing something close? Exploring cheaply or making the final? What must not change? Then it scores the allowed models on role, the deliverable's aspect ratio and length, sound, exact text and cost. Prices never come from the AI: in demo mode they are mock prices; with a Higgsfield key they come from the estimate endpoint.

SEARCH

Cheap and fast: many concepts, hooks and camera ideas before spending on finals.

CONTROL

Keeps what must not change: the truck, the product, the layout, the words, a movement.

SHIP

Premium output for the chosen direction only.

FINISH

Fixes and finishing: targeted edits, joining, platform versions, covers.
